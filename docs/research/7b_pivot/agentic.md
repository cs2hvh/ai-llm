# Agentic post-training: recipes, data, RL, benchmarks

## RECOMMENDATION
Adopt the OLMo 3 pipeline as your skeleton and the Nemotron 3 Nano recipe as your agentic upgrade path, and decide NOW to seed agency before post-training. Concretely: (1) put a code/math-heavy pretrain mix plus a 100-300B-token mid-training stage containing agentic trajectories (tool-call transcripts, OpenHands/SWE traces, terminal sessions, interleaved think+act data à la Youtu-LLM/AgentFounder) into the 7B pretraining plan — the evidence says post-training alone gets you BFCL-style function calling but not long-horizon SWE/terminal/deep-research agency; (2) post-train as SFT -> DPO -> RLVR: start from Dolci (unrestricted) + xLAM-60k (CC-BY-4.0) + ToolACE/Hermes-FC (Apache-2.0) + NVIDIA SWE-Zero/Nebius SWE-rebench trajectories (CC-BY-4.0) for SFT (~2-3M samples, dual /think-/no_think template like SmolLM3/Qwen3 fusion), DPO with ~150-250K pairs including WorkBench-style tool-verified preferences, then RLVR with a modified GRPO (clip-higher, token-level loss, no KL — copy OlmoRL or Nemotron configs) over ~100-350K verifiable tasks spanning math/code/IF/JSON/tool environments; (3) heavily exploit distillation — Qwen3 showed small models get ~90% of the benefit at 1/10 the cost, and Apache-2.0 teachers (Qwen3.5/3.6, gpt-oss-120b) plus 5M released Dolci completions make this compatible with a sovereign-model story if you document provenance.

Budget post-training at 5-15% of pretraining FLOPs (DeepSeek-R1's RL was ~5%; frontier is 15-25%) — on your B200/B300 fleet this is weeks, not months, and rollout throughput (vLLM/SGLang) will be the bottleneck, not training. The single biggest project risk is stack mismatch: every mature agentic RL framework (verl, SkyRL, PRIME-RL, NeMo-RL, slime) is PyTorch — your JAX/Keras3 codebase covers pretraining/SFT but not multi-turn RL. Either budget a weight-conversion bridge to run RLVR in verl (recommended: most mature, async, largest community) or accept Tunix's JAX-native but younger tooling; do not build a bespoke JAX agentic-RL stack solo. Set public 12-month targets against verified anchors: BFCL v3 >= 60, tau2-bench >= 45, IFEval >= 85, AIME-25 >= 60, LiveCodeBench >= 50, Terminal-Bench 2.0 >= 10, SWE-bench Verified 15-25% under an OpenHands scaffold — that lands you at Qwen3-8B-class agentic quality, which is a defensible, honest claim for a sovereign 7B in 2026.

# Agentic Post-Training for a 7B From-Scratch Model: Recipes, Data, RL, Benchmarks (research cut-off 2026-07-23)

## 1. Published post-training recipes for small agentic/reasoning models (2025–2026)

### OLMo 3 (Ai2, Nov 2025 — the most reproducible 7B recipe)
- Pipeline: **SFT -> DPO -> RLVR**, with two branches: **Think** (reasoning) and **Instruct** (fast/function-calling), all data released as the **Dolci** suite with "no license restrictions" ([Ai2 blog, Nov 2025](https://allenai.org/blog/olmo3); paper [arXiv:2512.13961, Dec 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.13961)).
- Data volumes (from HF dataset cards, accessed 2026-07-23): [Dolci-Instruct-SFT ≈ 2.15M samples](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/Dolci-Instruct-SFT), [Dolci-Think-DPO-7B = 150K preference pairs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/Dolci-Think-DPO-7B), [Dolci-Instruct-DPO ≈ 260K pairs](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/Dolci-Instruct-DPO), Dolci-Think-RL ≈ **100K RLVR prompts** across math/code/IF/chat ([Papers Explained 571, Jun 2026](https://ritvik19.medium.com/papers-explained-571-olmo-3-1ee7134a4e67)). A 5.03M-completion distillation set also exists ([Dolci-Think-RL-7B-Completions-SFT](https://huggingface.co/datasets/allenai/Dolci-Think-RL-7B-Completions-SFT)).
- RL algorithm: GRPO heavily modified — token-level loss, no KL, clip-higher, truncated importance sampling, no std-normalization, active sampling/refilling, PipelineRL-style async ("OlmoRL" in [open-instruct](https://github.com/allenai/open-instruct)) ([Interconnects, Nov 2025](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/olmo-3-americas-truly-open-reasoning); [Ritvik summary](https://ritvik19.medium.com/papers-explained-571-olmo-3-1ee7134a4e67)).
- Function-calling training: real trajectories collected via **MCP servers** (science QA, web search) + **SimFC** simulated trajectories built from **xLAM and ToolACE API pools**, with tool specs in system prompts and dedicated special tokens ([Ritvik summary of the Olmo 3 paper](https://ritvik19.medium.com/papers-explained-571-olmo-3-1ee7134a4e67)).
- Result anchors (7B): Olmo 3 Think 7B ≈ within 1–2 points of Qwen3-8B overall ([Interconnects](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/olmo-3-americas-truly-open-reasoning)); Olmo 3 Instruct 7B: **BFCL 49.8, LiveCodeBench v3 29.5, IFEval 85.6** ([Ai2 blog](https://allenai.org/blog/olmo3)). Olmo 3.1 (32B Think/Instruct) shipped early 2026 ([HF](https://huggingface.co/allenai/Olmo-3.1-32B-Think)).

### Qwen3 (May 2025) and the 2026 Qwen3.5/3.6 wave
- 4-stage pipeline for flagships: (1) long-CoT cold-start SFT; (2) reasoning RL with **GRPO on only 3,995 query–verifier pairs, ~170 steps**; (3) **thinking-mode fusion** (SFT mixing CoT + instruct data to give /think–/no_think dual mode); (4) general RL over ~20 task types incl. agent/tool use ([Qwen3 TR, arXiv:2505.09388, May 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.09388); [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)).
- Critical for a solo team: the small models (incl. 8B) were NOT given the full 4-stage pipeline — they were built by **strong-to-weak distillation (logit distillation) from the flagship at ~1/10 the GPU-hours** of the 4-stage method ([Qwen3 TR](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)).
- 2026: **Qwen3.5** open-weights family released 2026-02-16 (0.8B–397B-A17B incl. 9B dense) ([Codersera guide, 2026](https://codersera.com/blog/qwen-3-5-complete-guide-2026/)); **Qwen3.6-35B-A3B** (Apr 2026, Apache-2.0, hybrid Gated-DeltaNet/MoE, 262K ctx) posts **SWE-bench Verified 73.4, Terminal-Bench 2.0 51.5, TAU3-bench 67.2** and a "preserve thinking across turns" feature ([HF card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B)). This is the new bar for "small" agentic open models.

### NVIDIA Nemotron Nano 2 (9B, Aug 2025) and Nemotron 3 Nano (30B-A3B, Dec 2025)
- Nano 2 (9B dense hybrid Mamba): post-training ≈ **80–90B tokens**: 3 SFT stages (stage 2 dedicated to tool-calling; ~5% truncated-reasoning data for thinking-budget control), **IFEval-RL (16K prompts), DPO for tool-calling verified in a WorkBench environment, GRPO on HelpSteer3, RLHF**, then checkpoint merging (α=0.5) ([arXiv:2508.14444, Aug 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14444v2)). Scores: AIME-25 76.25, MATH-500 97.75, GPQA-D 64.48, LCB 70.79, IFEval 89.81, **BFCL v3 66.98**, RULER-128k 83.36.
- Nemotron 3 Nano (31.6B total / 3.2B active MoE, explicitly "for agentic reasoning"): **SFT on 18M samples at 256K seq len** (incl. conversational tool use, terminal use, SWE, 55 languages), then **multi-environment RLVR (synchronous GRPO + masked importance sampling; 128 prompts × 16 gens; envs: math 121K, code 22K, STEM QA 135K, JSON 9K, IF 49K, long-context QA 12K, agentic tool-use envs incl. a 690-task Workplace Assistant + 1K-task banking)**, then **RLHF with a generative reward model** (Qwen3-235B-Thinking GenRM, circular pairwise comparisons, group-relative length control) ([arXiv:2512.20848, Dec 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.20848); [HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.20848)). Scores: **BFCL v4 53.76, tau2-bench avg 49.04, SWE-bench (OpenHands) 38.76, Terminal-Bench-Hard 8.51, IFBench 71.51, AIME-25 89.06, LCB 68.25, RULER@1M 86.34**. NVIDIA released the SFT and RL datasets ([Nemotron docs](https://docs.nvidia.com/nemotron/nightly/nemotron/nano3/README.html)). Nemotron-Cascade 2 (Mar 2026) extends this with cascade RL + multi-domain on-policy distillation ([arXiv:2603.19220](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.19220v2)).

### SmolLM3 (3B, Jul 2025)
- **Mid-training on 140B reasoning tokens**, then SFT with a dual-mode chat template (/think, /no_think), then **APO** (anchored preference optimization, a DPO variant) with synthetic pairs (Qwen3-32B chosen vs Qwen3-0.6B rejected), plus model merging to recover long-context ([HF blog, Jul 2025](https://github.com/huggingface/blog/blob/main/smollm3.md); [model card](https://huggingface.co/HuggingFaceTB/SmolLM3-3B)). Full recipe + data open (Apache-2.0).

### gpt-oss (Aug 2025)
- 20B/120B Apache-2.0; post-training = **SFT + "high-compute RL" using the same CoT-RL process as o4-mini/o3**, with the **harmony** chat format, variable reasoning effort (low/med/high), and native tool use (browser, python, functions) ([model card, arXiv:2508.10925, Aug 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.10925); [OpenAI blog](https://openai.com/index/introducing-gpt-oss/)). gpt-oss-20b: AIME-25 91.7 (high, no tools), GPQA-D 71.5 (high), **tau-bench retail 54.8**. Recipe details (data volumes) are NOT published — inspiration only.

### DeepSeek-R1 distillation (Jan 2025)
- Small models trained by **SFT-only on 800K R1-generated samples (~600K reasoning via rejection sampling + 200K general), 2 epochs, no RL** — still the cheapest proven route to reasoning at 7B ([DeepSeek-R1 GitHub/HF, Jan 2025](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1); [distill-Qwen-7B card](https://huggingface.co/deepseek-ai/DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-7B)).

### Kimi K2 / K2 Thinking (Jul/Nov 2025) — the agentic-data playbook
- K2's agentic SFT data comes from a **simulation pipeline: 3,000+ real MCP tools scraped from GitHub + 20,000+ synthetic evolved tools**, hundreds of domains, generated agents + rubric-based tasks, multi-turn trajectories judged by an LLM against rubrics; joint RL combines **verifiable rewards + a self-critique rubric reward** ([arXiv:2507.20534, Jul 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20534v1); [Kimi blog](https://www.kimi.com/blog/kimi-k2)). K2 Thinking is **end-to-end trained to interleave CoT with 200–300 sequential tool calls** ([HF card, Nov 2025](https://huggingface.co/moonshotai/Kimi-K2-Thinking); [Interconnects, Nov 2025](https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k2-thinking-what-it-means)). Interleaved thinking (also MiniMax M2, [MacStories, Nov 2025](https://www.macstories.net/notes/on-minimax-m2-and-llms-with-interleaved-thinking-steps/)) is now a standard target behavior: think -> call tool -> read result -> think -> next call, with reasoning traces fed back每 turn (Qwen3.6 "preserve thinking" does the same, [HF](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-35B-A3B)).

## 3. RL for agents at 7B scale

**Frameworks.** The best current map is HuggingFace's survey of **16 async RL libraries** ([blog, 2026-03-10](https://huggingface.co/blog/async-rl-training-landscape)): **verl** is the production leader (19,673 stars; full async, save/resume for in-flight generations, FSDP+Megatron backends); **SLIME** (4.6K stars, Megatron, SGLang rollouts), **AReaL**, **PRIME-RL** (partial-rollout/group cancellation, FSDP2), **NeMo-RL** (in-flight continuation across weight updates), **SkyRL** (designed for multi-turn/long-horizon agent RL and SWE-bench-style tasks, [SkyRL-Agent arXiv:2511.16108](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2511.16108); [Anyscale overview](https://www.anyscale.com/blog/open-source-rl-libraries-for-llms)), **open-instruct/OlmoRL** (what Olmo 3 actually used), TRL (fine for DPO/SFT, weak for large-scale agentic RL), **Tunix (JAX-native, TPU-focused)**. Ray orchestration dominates (8/16). Disaggregated rollout/training with per-token staleness control is the key feature for agentic RL because trajectory lengths are wildly variable. Blackwell-class GPU support exists in vLLM/SGLang-based stacks.

**Algorithms.** GRPO is the base; **DAPO** adds clip-higher + dynamic sampling + token-level loss + overlong shaping; **GSPO** (Qwen) moves importance ratios to sequence level for stability, esp. MoE ([arXiv:2507.18071, Jul 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.18071); [comparative study arXiv:2512.07611](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.07611); [GRPO++ tricks](https://cameronrwolfe.substack.com/p/grpo-tricks)). Olmo 3 and Nemotron 3 both ship battle-tested GRPO-variant configs (see §1) you can copy verbatim.

**Reward design (proven patterns).** Code: unit-test execution pass rate (Nemotron code env, 22K tasks); math: exact-match verifiers; instruction following: rule-based checkers (IFEval-style, 46K+ tasks); JSON/structured output: schema validation; tool use: environment-verified end-state (WorkBench DPO in Nano 2; Workplace-Assistant RLVR env in Nemotron 3); open-ended: GenRM/LLM-judge with rubrics (Nemotron 3 RLHF; Kimi K2 self-critique rubric reward) ([arXiv:2512.20848](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.20848), [arXiv:2507.20534](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20534v1)).

**Compute cost of RL.** DeepSeek-R1's RL was ~**147K H800 GPU-hours vs 2.8M for V3 pretraining (~5%)** ([Digital Applied, 2026](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/post-training-revolution-rl-new-moat-2026)); frontier labs' post-training share grew to **~15–25% of total compute by 2025** ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/gradient-updates/r-and-d-vs-training-compute)); Qwen3's small-model distillation route costs **~1/10 of the 4-stage pipeline** ([Qwen3 TR](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.09388v1)). Plan 5–20% of pretraining FLOPs for post-training; wall-clock is dominated by rollout generation, not gradient steps.

## 5. Mid-training vs post-training: the evidence

The 2025-26 consensus moved decisively toward "agentic ability must be seeded before post-training":
- **Youtu-LLM (Tencent, 2B, Dec 2025)**: 4-stage pretraining ending in an **agentic mid-training stage that is ~60% agentic trajectories, >200B tokens of trajectory data** (Agentic-CoT 25B, math 20B, code 70B, deep-research 60B, tool/planning 25B); argues strong small-model agency requires "agent-oriented signals injected early and systematically" ([arXiv:2512.24618](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.24618)).
- **AgentFounder / Agentic Continual Pre-training (Alibaba, Sep 2025)**: post-training alone creates "optimization tensions" (learning behaviors while aligning); Agentic CPT (32K→128K ctx, long agentic-behavior corpora) yields AgentFounder-30B, first open model >30% HLE ([arXiv:2509.13310](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.13310); [Tongyi DeepResearch TR, arXiv:2510.24701](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.24701v1)).
- Every strong small model does a targeted mid-training: SmolLM3 **140B reasoning tokens** ([HF blog](https://github.com/huggingface/blog/blob/main/smollm3.md)); Olmo 3 **Dolmino 100B mid-training tokens** (math/science/code/IF) on a deliberately code/math-heavy 5.9T pretrain mix ([Ai2](https://allenai.org/blog/olmo3)); Nemotron pretrains include hundreds of billions of curated code/math tokens ([arXiv:2512.20848](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.20848)).
- Counterpoint: Nemotron Nano 2 built tool-calling **entirely in post-training** (no agentic data in the 20T pretrain) and still hit BFCL v3 67 ([arXiv:2508.14444](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.14444v2)) — so *function-calling format competence* is post-trainable, but *long-horizon agency* (deep research, 100+ step tool chains, terminal work) benefits strongly from mid-training exposure; MiroThinker also reports mid-training for "agentic atomic capabilities" ([arXiv:2603.15726](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.15726)). Note also RL data efficiency: Qwen3 needed only 3,995 RL pairs because the base already contained the knowledge — RL elicits, it does not create.

## OPEN QUESTIONS
- Exact size and mix of Dolci-Think-RL-7B and the GPU-hours Ai2 spent on the 7B RLVR stage (not disclosed in the blog or secondary sources; requires reading the full OLMo 3 paper PDF tables).
- Qwen3.5/3.6 post-training recipes: no technical report with stage-level data volumes had been published for the 2026 Qwen3.5 family as of 2026-07-23 — unclear whether the 9B is again distillation-only.
- Reliable GAIA and aider-polyglot scores for any 7-9B open model — effectively unpublished; unclear whether these should even be targets at 7B or left to scaffolded/frontier comparison.
- Whether Nemotron 3 Nano's released SFT (18M samples) and RL environment datasets are fully commercially licensed end-to-end (NVIDIA says 'accessible licenses' but per-subset terms need auditing before ingestion).
- Tunix (JAX-native RL) maturity for multi-turn tool-use environments — the HF survey covers it but I found no published end-to-end agentic RLVR run on it at 7B scale; the JAX-vs-PyTorch post-training bridge cost for this project is unquantified.
- How much agentic mid-training is enough at 7B: Youtu-LLM used >200B trajectory tokens at 2B params and Nemotron used none at 9B (post-training only) — no controlled scaling study isolates the minimum effective agentic mid-training dose for a 7B.
- Terminal/computer-use trace corpora remain thin: CLI-Gym/Tmax/TerminalTraj publish methods but large openly-licensed terminal trajectory datasets comparable to SWE-Zero do not yet exist.
- gpt-oss post-training data volumes and RL compute (OpenAI discloses process but no numbers), so its recipe can inspire but cannot be replicated.
