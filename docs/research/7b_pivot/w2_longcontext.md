## RECOMMENDATION
Build the 7B as a hybrid linear-attention model natively pretrained to >=262k: roughly 3:1 linear-to-full layers (Gated DeltaNet or a team-invented delta-rule/gating variant — this is exactly where the original-IP requirement can be earned, since Qwen3.5's GDN, Kimi's KDA, and Falcon's parallel-hybrid all differ in the gating design and all shipped within nine months), full-attention layers using GQA (8 KV heads) with NoPE or partial-RoPE plus learned attention sinks, trained with a 4k→32k(ABF)→128k→512k curriculum whose long phase costs only ~40–100B tokens (ProLong/Granite 4.1 pattern; train past 512k because Nemotron-H showed overshooting improves 128k quality), with torchtitan ring-attention CP handling the long stages on your B200/B300 clusters. This is now the cheapest PROVEN route to *native-quality* 250k+ in a 7B — Qwen3.5-9B, Falcon-H1R-7B, Kimi Linear, and Granite all landed it in the last 12 months — and it is the only route that simultaneously satisfies serving economics (~9.7 GB KV per 262k sequence vs ~38.7 GB full-GQA, i.e., ~18 vs ~4 concurrent agent sessions per B200), differentiation, and published-evidence grounding. Keep YaRN/DCA as a free 2–4x inference-extension lever on the full-attention layers, and treat trainable sparse (MoBA-style) as an optional later continued-training upgrade, not the base bet.

Before committing, run a wind-tunnel that directly targets the MiniMax failure mode: train matched ~1–2B models (full-GQA baseline vs your hybrid, identical data/muP/Muon setup) through the full curriculum AND a light SFT, then compare not perplexity but RULER@128k–256k, NoLiMa-style non-lexical retrieval, and multi-hop QA beyond 32k — MiniMax's deficits only appeared post-SFT at long range, so that is the gate. In the same wind-tunnel validate: (1) fp8-KV accuracy at 256k on your own needle/agentic evals (vLLM documented 128k–256k error accumulation); (2) that SGLang/vLLM can actually serve your custom linear layer on Blackwell (SGLang's KDA/GDN CuteDSL kernels are the template — budget kernel work if you deviate from GDN's state-update form); (3) long-doc data sufficiency, since >64k documents come almost only from code repos, books, and arXiv, and your Hindi/Indic sources will likely need Qwen2.5-1M-style synthetic long-range tasks (FIM, keyword retrieval, paragraph reordering) to fill the gap. If the hybrid loses the post-SFT multi-hop comparison, fall back to full GQA + fp8-KV + MLA-style compression — proven, but concede the differentiation to the data/agentic layer instead of the architecture.

## 1. How every relevant 2025–2026 model actually got long context

**Qwen3 / Qwen3-2507 (Alibaba).** The Qwen3-2507 refresh (July 2025) is the canonical "classic path at 256k": Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507 both declare a **native 262,144-token** context ([HF card, Jul 2025](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507); [30B card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-30B-A3B-Instruct-2507)). The recipe per the Qwen3 technical report ([arXiv 2505.09388, May 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2505.09388)): pretrain mostly at 4k, extend to 32k with **ABF** (RoPE base raised to 1M), then use **YaRN + Dual Chunk Attention for ~4x training-free inference extension**; the 2507 line adds a native long-context training phase to 262k. Notably, a **4B** model carries 262k native — small models can do this. The predecessor recipe, Qwen2.5-1M ([arXiv 2501.15383, Jan 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15383)), documents a **five-stage progressive length curriculum** starting at 4k, with **synthetic long-range tasks (fill-in-the-middle, keyword retrieval, paragraph reordering)** injected during pretraining, and agent-generated long-instruction data in post-training.

**Llama 4 Scout (Meta, Apr 2025).** Claims 10M context via **iRoPE** (interleaved NoPE layers + attention temperature scaling), but **no Llama 4 variant was trained beyond 256k**; the 10M is inference-time extrapolation, quality degrades above ~256k, and Meta published no RULER/NoLiMa evidence at long lengths ([HF release blog, Apr 2025](https://huggingface.co/blog/llama4-release); [community analysis](https://chatforest.com/reviews/meta-llama-4-scout-maverick-open-weight-llm-review/)). Treat 10M as marketing; the honest number is "trained to 256k."

**MiniMax (M1 → M2 → M2.5).** MiniMax-01 (456B-A45.9B) used a lightning-attention/softmax hybrid, **trained to 1M, extrapolating to 4M** ([arXiv 2501.08313, Jan 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08313)); M1 was the 1M-context hybrid reasoning model ([arXiv 2506.13585, Jun 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.13585)). Then the critical data point: **MiniMax M2 (Oct 2025) reverted to full attention.** Their own postmortem says hybrid/SWA variants matched full attention on standard benchmarks but showed **clear deficits in multi-hop reasoning at >32k context that only emerged after SFT and at scale**, and that linear/sparse infrastructure is immature and memory-bound ([MiniMax blog](https://www.minimax.io/news/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model); [LMSYS analysis, Nov 4, 2025](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2025-11-04-miminmax-m2/)). ## 2. The four routes, compared for a from-scratch 7B

**(a) Full GQA + RoPE-ABF/YaRN extension (classic).** Proven quality: Llama 3.1 used **six stages, ~800B tokens, 8k→128k** ([Llama 3 paper, arXiv 2407.21783](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.21783)); **ProLong** shows the long phase can be tiny — **40B tokens (20B@64k + 20B@512k) on top of Llama-3-8B produced SOTA 128k performance among 8B models, on 8x80GB GPUs** ([arXiv 2410.02660](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02660); [GitHub](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/ProLong)). Seed-OSS and Qwen3-2507 prove native 256–512k. Cheapest to train, zero architecture risk, best-understood evals — but worst to serve (Section 3) and **zero differentiation**.

**(b) SWA hybrid (Gemma-style 5:1).** Big KV savings (~6x, [Gemma 3 report](https://developers.googleblog.com/gemma-explained-whats-new-in-gemma-3/)), but no shipped >=250k model on this route; MiniMax explicitly reported **SWA variants performing significantly worse than full attention beyond 32k after SFT** ([MiniMax blog](https://www.minimax.io/news/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model)). Arcee Trinity Large (Jan 2026) uses 3:1 SWA + NoPE globals at 400B scale ([Raschka](https://magazine.sebastianraschka.com/p/a-dream-of-spring-for-open-weight)), but for 250k+ this route is under-evidenced.

**(c) Hybrid linear/SSM (GDN, KDA, Mamba-2, lightning).** Now the **most-validated route at 7B-class for 250k+**: Qwen3.5-9B/4B at 262k native (GDN 3:1), Falcon-H1R-7B at 256k, Kimi-Linear-48B-A3B (3B active) RULER@1M 94.8, Granite 4.x, Nemotron-H/3, MiniMax-01/M1 at 1M. Serving support matured in 2026: SGLang ships **CuteDSL KDA prefill kernels for Blackwell (SM100, 1.08–1.52x over Triton)**, int8 recurrent-state radix caching for KDA/GDN, and ReplaySSM fast decode ([SGLang releases](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/releases); [RFC #28511](https://github.com/sgl-project/sglang/issues/28511)); vLLM runs disaggregated serving for hybrid Nemotron 3 and lists **GDN (Qwen3.5+) on its roadmap** ([vLLM blog, Apr 21, 2026](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-04-21-hybrid-ssm-disagg)). The standing counter-evidence is MiniMax's M2 revert (multi-hop deficits, memory-bound kernels) — the risk is real but dated (their experience predates KDA/GDN maturity), and Kimi K3 (Jul 2026) plus Qwen3.5 represent two large labs betting the other way after MiniMax's warning.

**(d) Trainable sparse (NSA/DSA, MoBA).** Strong at flagship scale (V3.2, GLM-5), MoBA code is open and production-proven at Kimi ([GitHub](https://github.com/MoonshotAI/MoBA)), NSA showed perfect 64k NIAH on a 27B backbone with 260B tokens ([arXiv 2502.11089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.11089)). But there is **no open 7B-class from-scratch NSA/DSA model**, kernels (DSA "lightning indexer") are tuned for MLA-style stacks, and the sparse-index training dynamics add a research variable. Viable as a *second-phase* add-on (MoBA-style continued training from full attention), not as the primary bet.

## 3. KV-cache math at ~250k for a 7B (the serving forcing function)

Formula: **KV bytes = 2 (K,V) x n_layers x n_kv_heads x head_dim x seq_len x bytes/elt.**

Take a Qwen3-8B-like 7B: 36 layers, 8 KV heads, head_dim 128 (Qwen3-4B-2507 confirms 8 KV heads / 36 layers at 4B, [HF](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507)). Per token: 2x36x8x128x2B = **147,456 B ≈ 144 KiB/token (bf16)**. At 262,144 tokens:

- **Full GQA, bf16: 38.7 GB per sequence** (32-layer Llama-3-8B-style: 34.4 GB)
- **Full GQA, fp8-KV: 19.3 GB**
- **Gemma-style 5:1 SWA (1024 window): ~6.6 GB** (6 global layers full-length + 30 local layers x 1024 tokens ≈ 0.13 GB)
- **GDN/KDA 3:1 hybrid: ~9.7 GB bf16 / ~4.8 GB fp8** (9 full-attention layers) + O(100 MB) fixed recurrent state — matching Kimi's "up to 75% KV reduction" ([arXiv 2510.26692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692))
- **MLA (DeepSeek-style 576-dim latent): ~10.9 GB bf16** (576x2Bx36 = 40.5 KiB/token)

On a **B200 (192 GB)** with ~14 GB bf16 weights: full-GQA bf16 fits ~4 concurrent 262k sequences; fp8-KV ~9; the 3:1 hybrid ~18 bf16 / ~35 fp8. On **B300 (288 GB HBM3e, shipped Jan 2026,** [Vast.ai spec page](https://vast.ai/article/everything-you-need-to-know-about-the-nvidia-blackwell-ultra-b300)) roughly 1.55x those counts. For an *agentic* model whose economics depend on many concurrent long trajectories, this 4x KV difference is the whole business case. FP8-KV caveat: vLLM's study (Apr 22, 2026) finds fp8 KV gives 2x capacity with <=1–2 points degradation, but **errors concentrate at 128k–256k**, and one NIAH configuration regressed 91%→13% before accumulation fixes ([vLLM blog](https://vllm.ai/blog/2026-04-22-fp8-kvcache)) — validate on your own 256k evals.

## 4. Training practicalities

- **Curriculum norms:** 4k→32k (ABF to base ~1M) in main pretraining; long phase 32k→128k→256k(+512k) at the end. Llama 3.1: 6 stages / 800B tokens ([arXiv 2407.21783](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2407.21783)); Granite 4.1: 4k→32k→128k→512k inside a 15T-token run ([IBM blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ibm-granite/granite-4-1)); Qwen2.5-1M: five stages + synthetic long-range tasks ([arXiv 2501.15383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15383)). Budget: **20–100B tokens is the demonstrated range for a 7B long phase** (ProLong 40B; Llama's 800B was for 405B-scale). Nemotron-H's finding that 512k training improves 128k RULER argues for overshooting your 250k target to ~512k ([arXiv 2504.03624](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2504.03624)).
- **Context parallelism:** torchtitan's ring-attention CP is verified to **1M sequence length on Llama3-8B across 32 H100s**, composable with FSDP and torch.compile ([PyTorch forums](https://discuss.pytorch.org/t/distributed-w-torchtitan-breaking-barriers-training-long-context-llms-with-1m-sequence-length-in-pytorch-using-context-parallel/215082); [PyTorch CP tutorial](https://docs.pytorch.org/tutorials/unstable/context_parallel.html)). Ulysses requires num_heads >= sp_size ([HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/ulysses-sp)). Caveat for the hybrid route: ring/Ulysses apply to the attention layers; linear-layer sequence parallelism needs chunked-scan approaches (ZeCO, [arXiv 2507.01004](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.01004)) — but at 7B on B200s, a 256k batch fits with modest CP degrees anyway. ProLong trained 512k on 8x80GB using variable-length attention + memory-efficient CE ([GitHub](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/ProLong)).
- **Data:** genuinely long documents come almost entirely from **code repositories (repo-level concatenation), books, and arXiv** — ProLong's raw pool is ~2.7TB of exactly these ([GitHub](https://github.com/princeton-nlp/ProLong); mixtures released as [prolong-data-64K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/princeton-nlp/prolong-data-64K)/[512K](https://huggingface.co/datasets/princeton-nlp/prolong-data-512K)). Web text is short. Qwen2.5-1M supplements with **synthetic FIM, keyword-retrieval, and paragraph-reordering tasks**, and agent-generated long instructions ([arXiv 2501.15383](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15383)). Expect your 13-source multilingual corpus to contribute little beyond 32k for Hindi — plan synthetic long-range Indic tasks.

## 5. Long-context evals 2026: claimed vs effective

Standard set: **RULER** ([GitHub](https://github.com/NVIDIA/RULER)), **HELMET** (7 task categories to 128k, [arXiv 2410.02694](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.02694)), **LongBench v2** (503 MCQ, 8k–2M words; human experts 53.7%, best 2024-era model 50.1%, [arXiv 2412.15204](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.15204)), **NoLiMa** (non-lexical NIAH; **11 of tested models fall below 50% of their short-context baseline at 32k**, [arXiv 2502.05167](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.05167)), and **fiction.liveBench** (most models <80% by 192k; practical usable band often 16k–64k, [Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/context-windows); [analysis](https://danjcleary.substack.com/p/do-long-context-windows-actually)). Concrete gap example: Qwen3-30B-A3B claims 256k, scores 91.6 RULER@128k but has **effective context ~64k** ([llm-stats via search]; RULER methodology defines effective length against a Llama-2-7B@4k threshold). Meanwhile HELMET shows a 7B (MegaBeam-Mistral-7B, 512k) matching Llama-3.1-70B on long-context averages — parameter count is not the barrier; training is. Targets for this project: RULER@256k >= 85, NoLiMa-style non-lexical retrieval holding >= 85% of short baseline at 128k, HELMET full suite, plus agentic long-horizon evals (K2-Thinking-style multi-call stability).

## 6. Conclusion — cheapest proven route and the wind-tunnel

The cheapest route to a *working* 256k is (a): classic GQA + ABF + a 40–100B-token ProLong-style phase. But it fails both owner requirements: it is undifferentiated, and serving 250k agentic trajectories at 38.7 GB KV/sequence wrecks unit economics. The **hybrid linear 3:1 route (GDN/KDA-style) is now proven at exactly this scale** (Qwen3.5-9B 262k native, Falcon-H1R-7B 256k, Kimi Linear RULER@1M 94.8) with 4x KV savings and Blackwell kernels in SGLang/vLLM, and it leaves genuine IP room in the delta-rule/gating design — grounded novelty, not blind risk. MiniMax's M2 revert is the one serious counter-signal; it must be retired empirically in a wind-tunnel (matched 1–2B pairs, post-SFT multi-hop evals at >32k), not by argument.

- Exact token budgets of the long-context phases in Qwen3-2507/Qwen3.5 and Falcon-H1R (not disclosed) — the 40–100B figure is inferred from ProLong, Granite 4.1, and Llama 3.1, not from the closest analogues.
- Whether GDN/KDA hybrids hold up under heavy agentic RL post-training at 7B scale: Kimi K3 (2.8T MoE) and Kimi Linear's RL-scaling claims suggest yes, but no public matched ablation exists at 7B post-SFT/RL — this is exactly the MiniMax M2 failure mode and must be tested in-house.
- MiniMax M3's new sparse attention (15.6x long-context speedup teased) — details unreleased at research time; could change the sparse-attention calculus if open-sourced with kernels.
- Whether a Gemma 4 exists with a new long-context recipe — not found in any Jan–Jul 2026 source surveyed (UNVERIFIED absence).
- RULER/HELMET scores for Falcon-H1R-7B and Qwen3.5-9B specifically at 256k were not published in accessible sources — the effective-vs-claimed gap for these two closest comparables is unquantified.
