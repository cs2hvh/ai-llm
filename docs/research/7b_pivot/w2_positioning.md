## 1. The competitive field: what each small model does well, and the documented gaps

The "small agentic model" field moved fast in the last 8 months. The honest headline: **raw context length and basic tool calling are commoditizing even at small scale, but long-horizon agentic reliability, usable (not nominal) long context, and non-English agentic competence remain demonstrably unsolved.**

### The documented, evidence-backed gaps (all sizes, worse at 7B)

1. **Long-horizon agentic work is unsolved.** On OSWorld 2.0 (108 tasks, median human time 1.6 hours, 250+ steps), the best frontier system (Claude Opus 4.8) completes **20.6%**; the best open model tested (Qwen 3.7-Plus) completes **2.8%** ([arXiv:2606.29537](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.29537v1), 2026-06). Diagnostic work shows agents that hit 40–50% on short-horizon variants fall **below 10%** when the same task sits inside a longer interaction history — failure driven by losing the objective and looping, not retrieval ([arXiv:2604.11978](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11978), 2026-04).
2. **Nominal context ≠ usable context.** In long-context automated bug fixing with 100% retrieval recall guaranteed, **Qwen3-Coder-30B-A3B resolves only 7% at 64k and GPT-5-nano 0%**; agentic pipeline success comes from decomposition into short-context steps, not long-context reasoning ([arXiv:2602.16069](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.16069v1), 2026-02). LongFuncEval measures **7–91% degradation** in tool-call answer retrieval as tool responses grow, up to 85% degradation with large tool catalogs, and 13–40% across multi-turn ([arXiv:2505.10570](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.10570), 2025-04-30). Fiction.liveBench shows most models' deep comprehension decays from ~32k, with a practical usable band of 16–64k for most non-frontier models ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/benchmarks/fictionlivebench); [16x eval](https://eval.16x.engineer/blog/llm-context-management-guide), 2026).
3. **Small-model tool-calling judgment.** Community benchmarking of 21 local models found keyword-trap tool invocation, ignored negations ("don't check the weather"), and only 3/21 models correctly skipping a redundant tool call ([Local Agent Bench](https://mikeveerman.be/blog/github-2026-02-06-tool-calling-benchmark/), 2026-02-06). Multi-turn scores drop 5–10 points vs single-turn for every model on BFCL v4 ([Spheron BFCL guide](https://www.spheron.network/blog/tool-calling-benchmarks-bfcl-tau-bench-latency-optimization/), 2026-04).
4. **Non-English agentic is far behind.** On BFCL-Hi (Hindi function calling), the best score is **Gemma-3-27b-it at 62.42**; Sarvam-M scores **48.60**; a language-specialized 4B (Nemotron-4-Mini-Hindi) at 52.82 beats Llama-3.1-405B (49.53) — direct evidence that (i) Hindi tool use is weak everywhere and (ii) targeted training buys outsized gains ([arXiv:2508.19831](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.19831), 2025-08).

## 2. Wedge assessment

### (a) 250k+ context agentic automation — REAL GAP, but the moat is "usable," not "big"

Demand evidence: enterprises are deploying long-document/whole-repo agents and hitting the usable-context wall (papers above; BFCL v4's April 2026 pivot to 40%-weighted agentic evaluation reflects where buyers look — [BFCL](https://benchmarkingagents.com/best-benchmarks-for-tool-use/)). But **a config number of 250k is already table stakes**: Nemotron Nano (1M), Qwen3.6 (262K native), Granite 4.1 (512K family claim), Gemma 4 12B+ (256K). What NO ONE has, at any small size, is demonstrated task success at 250k in agentic settings — the 7%-at-64k result is the proof. A 7B that holds, say, >60% of its short-context agentic success rate at 250K would be genuinely differentiated and easily demonstrable. In the true 7–9B dense class the field is actually thin: Qwen3-8B (32K native), OLMo 3 (65K), Gemma 4 E4B (128K), Granite 8B (131K per Unsloth). Winning this wedge honestly requires long-horizon training methodology, not just RoPE tricks: horizon-reduction curricula, macro-actions, and subgoal decomposition are published and shown to stabilize small-model RL and produce horizon generalization ([arXiv:2605.02572](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.02572v1), 2026-05-04) — this aligns with the team's willingness to invent components, and the trajectory data + evals built for it are proprietary IP.

### (b) Sovereign / on-prem enterprise automation — REAL DEMAND, CONTESTED POSITION

Demand is quantified: on-prem/edge AI inference jumped to **55% of enterprise inference, from 12% in 2023** ([DreamFactory stats](https://www.dreamfactory.com/hub/on-premise-llm-deployment-statistics), 2026); on-prem held ~51.85% of LLM deployment share in 2025; Deloitte's State of AI 2026 (3,235 senior leaders, 24 countries) finds sovereign AI a board-level priority; Gartner projects >75% of European/ME enterprises "geopatriating" workloads by 2030 ([Arc Compute](https://www.arccompute.io/resources/arc-blog/private-llms-in-2026-the-infrastructure-decision-behind-enterprise-ai-control), 2026). Regulatory tailwind: **EU AI Act GPAI obligations applied from 2025-08-02 and Commission enforcement begins 2026-08-02** — 10 days from now — with a mandatory public training-data summary template ([WilmerHale](https://www.wilmerhale.com/en/insights/blogs/wilmerhale-privacy-and-cybersecurity-law/european-commission-releases-mandatory-template-for-public-disclosure-of-ai-training-data), 2025–2026; [AI Act guidelines](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/gpai-guidelines-overview/)). The team's existing governance artifacts and 13-source documented corpus are directly monetizable here. BUT IBM Granite already owns "ISO 42001-certified, signed, Apache 2.0 enterprise small model," and Nemotron ships through every enterprise vendor. A solo company cannot out-govern IBM; it can out-customize and out-exclusive it (see recommendation).

### (c) Indic-language agentic excellence — GENUINELY OPEN, WITH A CLOSING WINDOW

The sovereign Indian models are real but not agentic-first: at the India AI Impact Summit (Feb 2026) Sarvam unveiled a **30B model with a 32,000-token context** and a **105B flagship with 128,000-token context**, built around reasoning, voice, and Indian languages under IndiaAI Mission backing ([explainx](https://explainx.ai/blog/india-sovereign-ai-status-indiaai-mission-2026), 2026; [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/india-ai-impact-summit-2026-sovereign-models-sarvam-bharatgen-gnani-126021900417_1.html), 2026-02). Krutrim-3 trained on 2T+ tokens across 22 languages ([acecloud](https://acecloud.ai/blog/sarvam-ai-vs-chatgpt-gemini-krutrim-deepseek/), 2026-03); BharatGen covers 22 scheduled languages. None publish strong agentic/tool-use results; BFCL-Hi shows Sarvam-M at 48.60 vs Gemma-3-27b's 62.42. **Hindi/Indic long-context agentic tool use is an empty quadrant**: sovereign models have short contexts and weak function calling; global models have weak Hindi. The IndiaAI Mission (₹10,372 crore, 38,000+ subsidized GPUs, Application Development Initiative) funds exactly this category ([PIB](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleasePage.aspx?PRID=2012375&reg=3&lang=2), 2024; [inc42](https://inc42.com/features/indian-ai-startup-funding-soars-over-4x-yoy-in-h1-2026-but-is-it-enough-to-compete-globally/), 2026). The team's Sangraha-inclusive corpus is a head start. Risk: government-funded rivals can close this in 12–18 months; the defensible asset is the **eval + agentic training data**, not the language coverage.

Computer use requires vision (7B text-only can't play), and even frontier systems get 20.6% on long-horizon OSWorld 2.0. RPA wasn't replaced; it was absorbed into a platform war (Microsoft, UiPath, ServiceNow, IBM...) where UiPath already distributes Nemotron ([Windows Forum analysis](https://windowsforum.com/threads/2026-enterprise-ai-automation-the-agent-platform-war-behind-rpa-copilots-governance.424066/), 2026-06; [reworked](https://www.reworked.co/digital-workplace/uipaths-business-automation-vision-shifts-from-rpa-to-agentic-ai/)). Lesson: open weights drive distribution; money is made on deployment, exclusivity contracts, and verticals.

On "exclusivity": pure API-only maximizes IP control but a solo unknown lab gets zero adoption that way in a world where Gemma 4 and Granite are Apache 2.0. The workable structure is **revenue-gated open weights (Liquid-style) + paid exclusivity carve-outs** (e.g., exclusive vertical or geographic licenses for the Indic-agentic capability) + in-VPC deployment contracts where the team's provenance/governance documentation is the compliance deliverable that closes the deal after 2026-08-02.
