# Teachers + distillation strategy for a 7B student

## RECOMMENDATION
Adopt the Qwen3 post-training distillation pipeline, not the Gemma full-pretraining one. Concretely: (1) pretrain the 7B normally on your 13-source corpus; (2) run decay-phase-only logit KD over the final ~100-300B tokens using offline top-K (K=64) caches from Qwen3-235B-A22B or Qwen3.5-397B-A17B — this fits the existing ~$4,100 budget line (roughly 700-2,000 B200-hours + 32-96 TB of cache, essentially free on your own clusters) and is the proven MobileLLM-R1/Minitron pattern; (3) do sequence-level SFT on permissive trace data only — OpenThoughts3-1.2M (Apache 2.0), Nemotron-Post-Training v2/v3 (CC-BY-4.0, and the v3 agentic/tool-calling subsets are the best open agentic traces available), and Dolci (ODC-BY) — explicitly excluding AM-DeepSeek-R1-Distilled (CC-BY-NC); top up agentic/tool-use coverage with fresh traces from DeepSeek-V4 (MIT, explicit distillation permission, ~$1-5k for 5B output tokens) and Kimi K2.x/GLM-5.x for trajectory diversity; (4) replace most of the RL budget with on-policy distillation (reverse-KL GKD, TRL GOLD trainer or Tinker-style loop) against a large teacher — the Qwen3 report and Thinking Machines both show this beats RL at ~1/10 the compute, which matters enormously for a solo-led project.

Make the tokenizer call deliberately and early: if the 131k SPM-Unigram tokenizer is not a hard sovereignty requirement, switch to the Qwen3 tokenizer — it converts the strongest permissively-licensed teacher family into drop-in logit teachers for both stages and eliminates all cross-tokenizer machinery; if sovereignty wins, keep your tokenizer, accept sequence-level KD as the workhorse, and use GOLD (in TRL, Oct 2025) for cross-tokenizer on-policy distillation, treating offline cross-tokenizer logit caching as not worth the complexity. Avoid Llama 4 outputs entirely (forced "Llama" naming), avoid MiniMax M3 (new restrictive community license), and prefer Gemma 4 over Gemma 3 if Google models are used at all (Apache 2.0 vs. flow-down terms). Total realistic distillation spend on owned B200/B300 hardware: $5-25k of opportunity-cost GPU time plus ≤$10k of external API generation — an order of magnitude less than the quality-equivalent RL program.

## 2. Technique evidence, 2025-2026, and the scale it was proven at

**Logit KD throughout pretraining.** Proven at scale by Gemma 2 (2B/9B distilled from 27B, [arXiv 2408.00118](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.00118v1), 2024) and Gemma 3 ([arXiv 2503.19786](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.19786), 2025-03), and by Llama 3.2 1B/3B, where "logits from the Llama 3.1 8B and 70B models were incorporated into the pre-training stage... used as token-level targets" ([Meta blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/llama-3-2-connect-2024-vision-edge-mobile-devices/), 2024-09). **Gemma 4's technical report ([arXiv 2607.02770](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.02770), 2026-07-02) does not mention distillation at all** — neither abstract nor training section on my read; third-party blogs claiming Gemini-teacher logit KD for Gemma 4 are UNVERIFIED. So the strongest current open releases (Qwen3, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi) do *not* run logit KD through full pretraining for their student models — they pretrain normally and distill later.

**Mid-training / decay-phase KD.** MobileLLM-R1 (Meta, [arXiv 2509.24945](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.24945), 2025-09) applies logit KD from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct **during the mid-training phase while LR decays linearly to zero** — exactly the "decay-phase-only KD" pattern — producing sub-billion reasoners that match models trained on far more tokens. NVIDIA's Minitron line (prune + KD continued-pretraining on ~100B tokens) is the other repeated demonstration ([NVIDIA blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/how-to-prune-and-distill-llama-3-1-8b-to-an-nvidia-llama-3-1-minitron-4b-model/), 2024, still the production recipe behind Nemotron Nano models; see also `nvidia/Puzzle-KD-Nemotron-Post-Training-Dataset-v2` on HF).

**Compute-optimality.** Apple's *Distillation Scaling Laws* ([arXiv 2502.08606](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.08606), 2025-02, v2 2025): with an **existing teacher**, distillation beats supervised pretraining **up to a compute level that scales predictably with student size**; beyond it, plain pretraining wins; if you must also train the teacher, supervised learning is generally preferable. Practical translation for a 7B trained on trillions of tokens on owned B200s: you are likely *past* the crossover for full-pretraining KD, but decay-phase KD sits in the regime where the teacher's signal is still strongly beneficial per FLOP.

**Sequence-level KD (SFT on teacher traces).** The dominant, cheapest technique; evidence at 7B is overwhelming: OpenThinker3-7B (SFT-only on OpenThoughts3) hits AIME'24 69.0, AIME'25 53.3, MATH500 90.0 ([HF dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-thoughts/OpenThoughts3-1.2M), 2025-06), beating all similarly-sized open-data reasoning models at the time.

**On-policy distillation (GKD family).** The 2025-2026 breakout technique. Thinking Machines ([blog](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/on-policy-distillation/), 2025-10-27): Qwen3-32B teacher → Qwen3-8B-Base student, per-token **reverse-KL on student-sampled trajectories**; AIME'24 60%→70% on ~77k prompts × 4 samples (~2.5B sampled tokens, ~150 steps, ~1,800 GPU-hr vs 17,920 for RL); **9-30× cheaper than off-policy distillation, 50-100× more compute-efficient than RL**. The Qwen3 Technical Report ([arXiv 2505.09388](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.09388), 2025-05) institutionalized this: all small Qwen3 models (0.6B-14B, 30B-A3B) were produced by "strong-to-weak distillation" — off-policy response distillation then **on-policy logit alignment against Qwen3-32B/235B** — beating their own RL runs **at 1/10 the GPU hours** with better pass@64. This is the single most relevant precedent for your 7B.

**Cross-tokenizer KD.** Rapidly maturing: ULD (optimal-transport logit alignment, Boizard et al. 2024/2025), MultiLevelOT ([arXiv 2412.14528](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.14528)), ALM + tokenizer transplant in [tokenkit](https://github.com/bminixhofer/tokenkit) (April 2025), **GOLD — General Online Logit Distillation — merged into Hugging Face TRL** ([TRL docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/trl/main/en/gold_trainer), Oct 2025), which extends on-policy distillation to any teacher/student tokenizer pair and beat ULD and GRPO on math; and 2026 papers (X-Token [arXiv 2605.21699](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.21699); DPCA "Breaking the Tokenizer Barrier" [arXiv 2606.09456](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.09456v1), 2026-06-08, Qwen3-8B→Llama-3.1-8B +5.6 avg points at 4% of SFT-extrapolation compute). Verdict: cross-tokenizer on-policy KD **works** at 7-8B scale, but every result shows extra machinery and residual loss versus same-tokenizer reverse-KL; it is a fallback, not the plan.

## 3. Cost/benefit at 7B: full-pretraining KD vs decay-phase KD vs post-training-only

Teacher forward pass ≈ 2 × active-params FLOPs/token. On B200 (FP8 dense ≈ 4.5 PFLOPS, ~40% util), my estimates:

- **Full-pretraining online KD** (e.g., 10T tokens, Qwen3.5-A17B teacher): ~52,000+ B200-hr of teacher forward alone, roughly **doubling step cost** of a 7B pretrain (student fwd+bwd ≈ 42 GFLOPs/tok vs teacher fwd 34 GFLOPs/tok). Only Google does this, with free internal teachers; Apple's scaling laws say the benefit shrinks precisely in your high-token regime. **Not recommended.**
- **Decay-phase-only offline top-K logit KD** (100-300B tokens): Qwen3-235B-A22B ≈ **680-2,040 B200-hr** ($2.0k-10.2k at $3-5/hr market; near-zero marginal cost on owned clusters). Qwen3.5-A17B ≈ 525-1,575 hr. Storage for top-64 logits ≈ **32 TB per 100B tokens** (fp16 value + 3-byte index). B200 market rates July 2026: $2.69-6.01/hr typical ([Thunder Compute](https://www.thundercompute.com/blog/nvidia-b200-pricing), [getdeploying](https://getdeploying.com/gpus/nvidia-b200)). **The old $4,100 budget is still exactly right for a 100B-token top-K cache at market prices, and effectively free on your own B200/B300 clusters.**
- **Post-training-only distillation**: sequence-level SFT on free permissive datasets ($0) + on-policy GKD (~2-5k B200-hr ≈ $6-25k rented, marginal on owned). Teacher scoring is prefill-only and trivial (~30-60 B200-hr per 2.5B sampled tokens for a 40B-active teacher). If instead generating fresh traces via API: 5B output tokens costs ~$1.2k (DeepSeek V4 Flash @ $0.242/M out), ~$4.4k (V4 Pro @ $0.87/M), ~$12.5k (GLM-5.2 @ $2.508/M), ~$17k (Kimi K2.6 @ $3.41/M) — [OpenRouter pricing](https://openrouter.ai/moonshotai/kimi-k2.6) (June 2026).

Evidence-weighted verdict: **post-training distillation (sequence-level + on-policy) carries ~80% of the capability lift for ~5% of the cost; decay-phase logit KD is a cheap, proven add-on; full-pretraining KD is a Google-only luxury.**

## 5. Same-tokenizer-as-teacher: worth engineering for?

Yes — it is the highest-leverage architecture decision in this dimension. Every headline result (Qwen3 strong-to-weak, TML on-policy, MobileLLM-R1 — which deliberately adopted the Llama tokenizer to match its teacher) used shared vocabularies. Cross-tokenizer methods (GOLD/ULD/ALM/DPCA) exist and are integrated into TRL, but add engineering surface and measurable slack — the opposite of your "proven stack" mandate. Adopting the Qwen3 tokenizer (151,669-token BBPE) makes Qwen3-235B-A22B and Qwen3.5-A17B drop-in logit teachers for both decay-phase KD and on-policy GKD. The cost is abandoning the 131k SPM-Unigram sovereign tokenizer; the middle path (tokenkit-style transplant of a teacher onto your vocab) is viable but research-grade.

## 6. What the strongest 2026 practitioners actually do (pattern to copy)

Pretrain normally → mid-train on high-quality data with (optional) logit KD in the decay phase → SFT on verified teacher traces → on-policy distillation against a big teacher (replacing most RL) → small targeted RL only where verifiable rewards exist. This is the Qwen3 pipeline, validated independently by Thinking Machines, and it is 10-100× cheaper than RL-heavy post-training.
