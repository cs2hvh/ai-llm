# Where small labs have built genuine original IP (2024-2026 evidence)

## RECOMMENDATION
Rank 1 — Proprietary agentic data engine (highest capability delta, only surface that stays secret with open weights). The 2025-26 evidence is unambiguous: Kimi K2's tool-use dominance came from a synthetic-trace pipeline (3K real MCP tools → 20K+ synthetic → rubric-filtered trajectories), and NVIDIA's Nemotron releases confirm agentic data is the scarce asset. Build a K2-style engine plus verified RL environments, extended with Indic-language agentic traces nobody else has (leveraging the existing 13-source corpus and Sangraha) — that combination does not exist anywhere. Effort: ~2 people, 4-6 months to first 1M-trajectory corpus, then continuous; runs on inference-class capacity of the existing B200 fleet. Keep the pipeline as trade secret; open the weights. Rank 2 — A named hybrid long-context architecture as the visible originality layer: a KDA/GDN-family linear block with your own gating/memory twist, interleaved 3:1 with full attention (Kimi Linear's proven layout; MiniMax M2's retreat says do NOT go fully linear), tuned via MiniCPM-style muP wind-tunnel ablations the team already knows how to run, targeting verified RULER/BABILong quality at 256K-512K — a slot no open 7B credibly holds. Effort: ~2 people, 3-4 months of sub-1B ablations before committing the 7B run; the named block + distinct config.json model_type + a technical report with fair-comparison ablations is exactly how LFM2/Granite 4/Kimi Linear earned "original" credit. Rank 3 — Indic-optimized SuperBPE-style tokenizer (+ provenance-tracked Indic corpus): cheapest surface (~1 person, 4-8 weeks), evidence-backed (+4% avg downstream, 27% inference savings, 2.2x lower cross-language token variance), compounds with both other surfaces, and is an immediately visible not-a-copy marker. For exclusivity, copy Liquid's structure, not Meta's: open weights under a revenue-gated LFM-style license, data engine and RL environments private, publish the architecture ablations for credibility, and skip patents except possibly one defensive filing on the data-engine method. Do not pursue pure-architecture novelty (RWKV/xLSTM path: citations, no ecosystem) and do not bet the company on the Indic-sovereign label alone — Sarvam holds that flag; beat them on the axis they lack (agentic + >=250K verified context).

## FULL REPORT
# Where Small Labs Built Genuine Original IP (2024–2026): Evidence Review for a ~7B Agentic Model

Research date: 2026-07-23. All facts grounded in live sources fetched today; items I could not confirm live are marked UNVERIFIED.

## 1. Case studies: what was actually original, and did it work?

### Zyphra (Zamba/Zamba2) — architecture originality at 7B on a small budget
- IP claim: Mamba2 backbone + **two shared attention blocks** interleaved ABAB, with LoRA projectors on shared MLP blocks so shared blocks specialize per depth — a genuinely distinctive layout, not a Llama clone ([Zamba2-7B blog](https://www.zyphra.com/post/zamba2-7b), Oct 2024; [Zamba paper, arXiv 2405.16712](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.16712)).
- Evidence it mattered: Zyphra claims Zamba2-7B beat Llama3-8B/Mistral-7B/Gemma-class peers with 25% faster time-to-first-token, ~20% higher tokens/sec, trained on **128 H100s for ~50 days** on 3T tokens (Zyda + open data) — their explicit thesis: "at the 7B scale the frontier is still reachable and surpassable with a small team and moderate budget" ([zyphra.com/post/zamba2-7b](https://www.zyphra.com/post/zamba2-7b)).
- Payoff: Zyphra became a **$1B-valuation unicorn in Oct 2025** with IBM and AMD investing; employee counts reported between 44 and 72 ([Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/zyphra/__dgBOZCOyEsb5KMZEfcLsff9-nH8LVHOLIhWAT4sHjzY), [PitchBook](https://pitchbook.com/profiles/company/437883-40), 2026). The core research team behind Zamba was far smaller than total headcount (UNVERIFIED exact size).

### Liquid AI (LFM2) — architecture found by search, monetized by license
- IP claim: hybrid of **gated short convolution blocks + a minority of GQA blocks (1:3 GQA:conv ratio)** discovered via **hardware-in-the-loop architecture search** under edge latency/memory constraints ([LFM2 Technical Report, arXiv 2511.23404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23404), Nov 28 2025; [LFM2 launch blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/liquid-foundation-models-v2-our-second-series-of-generative-ai-models), 2025).
- Evidence: family 350M–8.3B trained on **10–12T tokens**; claims ~2x faster CPU prefill/decode than similar-size models; LFM2-2.6B: 79.56 IFEval, 82.41 GSM8K ([arXiv 2511.23404](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.23404)). Scaled up to LFM2-24B-A2B MoE in Feb 2026 ([Liquid blog](https://www.liquid.ai/blog/lfm2-24b-a2b)). Real ecosystem pull: llama.cpp added a dedicated LFM2 architecture ([llama.cpp issue #22287](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/22287)).
- Monetization model: **"LFM Open License"** — Apache-2.0-like but commercial rights terminate above $10M annual revenue; paid license above that ([liquid.ai/lfm-license](https://www.liquid.ai/lfm-license), [docs](https://docs.liquid.ai/lfm/help/model-license)). This is the cleanest small-lab template for "open-ish weights + retained exclusivity."

### TII Falcon (Falcon-H1, Falcon-E) — hybrid-head + extreme quantization
- Falcon-H1 (July 2025): **parallel** attention+Mamba2 heads (outputs concatenated before projection, tunable head ratio), RoPE base frequency 10^11 for long-context generalization, depth-over-width findings; 0.5B–34B family claimed parameter efficiency rivaling Qwen2.5-72B/Llama3.3-70B ([arXiv 2507.22448](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.22448); [falcon-lm.github.io/blog/falcon-h1](https://falcon-lm.github.io/blog/falcon-h1/)).
- Falcon-Edge (May 2025): 1B/3B **ternary BitNet (1.58-bit)** models, fine-tunable, ~5x CPU speedups typical of BitNet-class models ([falcon-lm.github.io/blog/falcon-edge](https://falcon-lm.github.io/blog/falcon-edge/); [HF blog](https://huggingface.co/blog/tiiuae/falcon-edge)). TII is state-funded, not 2–5 people, but the specific hybrid ablation methodology is reproducible by a small team.

### ModelBest/OpenBMB (MiniCPM) — methodology as IP
- IP claim: **"model wind tunnel"** — hyperparameters searched on <100M models, transferred via muP + batched scaling laws; **WSD scheduler** enabling continuous training and cheap scaling-law measurement; found compute-optimal data:model ratio ~**192x** vs Chinchilla's 20x ([arXiv 2404.06395](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.06395), Apr 2024).
- Continued: MiniCPM4 (June 2025) added **InfLLM v2 trainable sparse attention** — 5% attention density at 128K, 100% RULER-NIAH at 128K with YaRN, 7x speedup vs Qwen3-8B on Jetson AGX Orin ([arXiv 2506.07900](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.07900); [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2025/06/16/openbmb-releases-minicpm4-ultra-efficient-language-models-for-edge-devices-with-sparse-attention-and-fast-inference/)). Feb 2026: MiniCPM-SALA hybridizes sparse + linear attention ([arXiv 2602.11761](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2602.11761)). This lab is the closest existence proof that a muP-fluent mid-size team converts *training methodology* into durable reputation and models that punch above weight.

### Nous Research — data recipes + distributed optimizer
- IP claim: DisTrO/DeMo optimizers cutting inter-GPU communication by **3–4 orders of magnitude** (DeMo co-developed with Adam co-author Diederik Kingma) ([NousResearch/DisTrO](https://github.com/NousResearch/DisTrO)); Hermes post-training data recipes.
- Evidence: Hermes 4.3 (2026) post-trained entirely on the Psyche distributed network at ~144k tokens/sec across 24 nodes, matching centralized throughput ([nousresearch.com/introducing-hermes-4-3](https://nousresearch.com/introducing-hermes-4-3)); Paradigm invested at a reported $1B valuation ([Gate News](https://www.gate.com/news/detail/nous-research-deep-dive-a-decentralized-ai-lab-that-paradigm-invested-in-at-20290685), UNVERIFIED valuation detail).

### Prime Intellect — distributed training at scale
- INTELLECT-1 (10B, Dec 2024, [arXiv 2412.01152](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2412.01152)) through **INTELLECT-3: 106B MoE trained on 512 H200s with large-scale RL**, strong math/code results for size ([arXiv 2512.16144](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16144v1), Dec 2025). IP = infra/recipes for decentralized RL, not architecture.

### Moondream — niche as IP, near-solo team
- Tiny VLM (0.5B/2B) built essentially by one researcher (Vik Korrapati) then a 2-founder company (M87 Labs, $4.5M pre-seed, Oct 2024: Felicis, M12 GitHub Fund, Ascend) ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2024/seattle-startup-moondream-led-by-aws-vets-raises-4-5m-for-vision-language-model-software/)). Differentiation = capability niche: zero-shot detection/pointing and UI grounding (ScreenSpot F1@0.5 60.3→80.4) with ~5M monthly HF downloads ([Roboflow](https://blog.roboflow.com/moondream-2/); [moondream.ai](https://moondream.ai/blog/introducing-moondream-0-5b); download figure UNVERIFIED precisely).

### RWKV / NXAI xLSTM — architecture originality, limited commercial pull
- RWKV-7 "Goose" (Mar 2025): dynamic state evolution, SoTA-at-3B multilingual claims, can recognize all regular languages (beyond standard-conjecture transformer capability), 3.1T-token open multilingual corpus, Apache 2.0 ([arXiv 2503.14456](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.14456)). Community-driven; adoption is real but niche.
- xLSTM 7B (Mar 2025): comparable downstream quality to similar 7B LLMs with much faster recurrent inference ([arXiv 2503.13427](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2503.13427); [NXAI](https://www.nx-ai.com/en/news/xlstm-7b-nxai-releases-its-new-xlstm-7b-model)). Lesson: pure-alternative architectures earn citations, not ecosystems; hybrids (Zamba, Falcon-H1, Granite 4, Kimi Linear) earn both.

### Arcee, Pleias, AI2, Reka, Mistral (brief)
- **Arcee**: MergeKit ([arXiv 2403.13257](https://arxiv.org/abs/2403.13257)) + AFM-4.5B built with merging/distillation/spectrum training; differentiation is tooling + enterprise data-efficiency, extended AFM to 64K context post-hoc ([arcee.ai blog](https://www.arcee.ai/blog/deep-dive-afm-4-5b-the-first-arcee-foundational-model)). Merging = real technique, weak moat (toolkit is open).
- **Pleias**: Common Corpus — 2.0T permissively-licensed tokens with **document-level provenance**, 30+ languages ≥1B tokens each; ICLR 2026 oral; 1.2B models comparable to models trained on non-permissive data ([HF dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PleIAs/common_corpus); [arXiv 2506.01732](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.01732)). Provenance-as-IP works for regulated-market positioning.
- **AI2 OLMo 3** (Nov 20 2025): differentiation via radical openness — full "model flow" (data, every checkpoint, logs), 7B/32B, first fully-open 32B thinking model, 65K context, Apache 2.0 ([allenai.org/blog/olmo3](https://allenai.org/blog/olmo3)). Openness earns adoption/credibility, by construction zero exclusivity.
- **Reka**: ~57 employees, from-scratch multimodal family, $110M at >$1B valuation (July 2025, NVIDIA/Snowflake) ([reka.ai news](https://reka.ai/news/reka-secures-110-million-to-accelerate-adoption-of-its-multimodal-ai-platforms); [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/reka/__CA6OEk4c0RuHQVTD-0aphtcEGYBgPNDXjnl1KTJS7mw)).
- **Mistral 7B** (Oct 2023): sliding-window attention + GQA at 7B — a modest architectural tweak plus excellent execution beat Llama2-13B and created a company; the tweak itself was quickly commoditized ([HF docs](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/en/model_doc/mistral)).

### The two most important 2025–26 data points for architecture risk
- **Kimi Linear / KDA** ([arXiv 2510.26692](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.26692), Oct 2025): Kimi Delta Attention (finer-grained channel-wise gating over Gated DeltaNet) in a **3:1 KDA:full-attention hybrid** — first linear-hybrid claimed to beat full attention under fair comparisons across short-context, long-context, and RL regimes; **75% KV-cache reduction, up to 6x decode throughput at 1M context**. It graduated into **Kimi K3 (July 16 2026): 2.8T-param MoE, KDA + 1M context**, weights promised by July 27 2026 ([MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/07/16/moonshot-ai-releases-kimi-k3-a-2-8-trillion-parameter-open-moe-model-with-kimi-delta-attention-and-1m-context/)).
- **MiniMax M2's retreat to full attention** ([minimax.io](https://www.minimax.io/news/why-did-m2-end-up-as-a-full-attention-model), Oct–Nov 2025; [LMSYS analysis](https://www.lmsys.org/blog/2025-11-04-miminmax-m2/)): linear attention is memory-bound in training, numerically fragile with low-precision KV caches, and clashes with prefix caching/speculative decoding in production. Full attention "wins because it's the least fragile choice" for code/agents/RL. Architecture novelty at a small lab must be hedged (keep periodic full-attention layers).

## 2. Viable IP surfaces for a 2–5 person team — defensibility assessment

1. **Architecture variants (hybrid layouts, memory modules, new blocks)** — *Produced real deltas*: Zamba2, Falcon-H1, LFM2, Kimi Linear, Granite 4 (9:1 Mamba2:transformer, best-in-class IFEval for size; dubbed "Western Qwen" by [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/ai/western-qwen-ibm-wows-with-granite-4-llm-launch-and-hybrid-mamba-transformer), Oct 2025). Defensibility: LOW once weights ship (architecture is visible in config.json and reimplementable), but HIGH as *originality signal*. Risk is real (MiniMax M2); hybrids with retained full attention are the proven safe zone.
2. **Data engines** — *The strongest capability deltas of 2025–26 came from here*: Kimi K2's agentic pipeline (≈3,000 real MCP tools → 20,000+ synthetic tools → generated agents/tasks/trajectories → rubric-judge rejection sampling) is the canonical recipe for agentic excellence ([K2 tech report, arXiv 2507.20534](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.20534)); NVIDIA validated the surface by open-sourcing 10T+ pretraining tokens, 40M post-training samples, 12 RL environments, ~900K tool/reasoning tasks ([HF: Data for Agents](https://huggingface.co/blog/nvidia/open-data-for-agents); [NVIDIA blog](https://developer.nvidia.com/blog/inside-nvidia-nemotron-3-techniques-tools-and-data-that-make-it-efficient-and-accurate/), Dec 2025). Defensibility: **HIGH — the pipeline and raw data never ship with open weights**. Pleias shows provenance itself can be the differentiator.
3. **Training algorithms (optimizer/schedule/curriculum/RL recipes)** — Real (MiniCPM WSD/wind-tunnel; Muon; DisTrO/DeMo), but defensibility LOW-to-MEDIUM: once published, copied in weeks; if unpublished, no credibility benefit. Works best as reputation IP (papers) layered on a private data moat. Nous shows optimizer IP can define a company only when tied to an infrastructure story (decentralized training).
4. **Capability niches** — Moondream (UI grounding/detection at 2B), MegaBeam (AWS small team pushed Mistral-7B to 512K, only open model competitive on BABILong at 512K without RAG — [HF model card](https://huggingface.co/aws-prototyping/MegaBeam-Mistral-7B-512k), [Amazon Science paper, arXiv 2505.08651](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.08651v1), May 2025). Defensibility: MEDIUM — niche leadership persists while incumbents ignore the niche. Indic caveat: **Sarvam already shipped from-scratch sovereign models Feb 2026 — Sarvam 30B MoE (~2.4B active, 65K ctx) and Sarvam 105B MoE (~10B active, 128K ctx), Apache 2.0, 22 languages** ([sarvam.ai](https://www.sarvam.ai/blogs/indias-sovereign-llm); [buildfastwithai](https://www.buildfastwithai.com/blogs/sarvam-105b-india-s-open-source-llm-for-22-indian-languages-2026)), but a Forbes review notes India "still cannot prove it works" on evaluation ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/janakirammsv/2026/03/07/india-can-train-a-sovereign-model-but-still-cannot-prove-it-works/), Mar 2026). Note the ceiling: **no Indic model at ≥250K context; Sarvam tops out at 128K.**
5. **Tokenizer innovations** — Underrated and cheap: SuperBPE (COLM 2025) delivers **+4.0% average across 30 tasks (+8.2% MMLU) with 27% less inference compute** at fixed model/vocab/train-compute, and up to 33% fewer tokens per text; multilingual token-count variance across 97 languages drops up to 2.2x ([arXiv 2503.13423](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.13423); [superbpe.github.io](https://superbpe.github.io/)). For Indic scripts (historically token-inefficient), this compounds. Defensibility LOW (algorithm public) but a custom vocab is a visible, cheap "not-a-copy" marker.

## 3. Signaling "not a copy" credibly

What the community actually credited 2025–26: (a) **a technical report with ablations** — Falcon-H1's and LFM2's method sections and Kimi Linear's fair-comparison ablations drove coverage far more than benchmarks alone; (b) **a distinct `model_type`/architecture requiring dedicated inference support** — llama.cpp adding LFM2 ([issue #22287](https://github.com/ggml-org/llama.cpp/issues/22287)) and Kimi's open KDA kernels are unfakeable originality proofs; (c) **a named, reusable component** (KDA, InfLLM v2, Arcee Fusion, WSD) that others adopt; (d) unique eval wins in a legible niche (Granite 4 on IFEval; MegaBeam on BABILong-512K; Moondream on ScreenSpot). Conversely, third parties can fingerprint derivative weights (REEF, [arXiv 2410.14273](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.14273)), so "started from Qwen" claims eventually surface — training from scratch with a distinct block layout and tokenizer is the only clean answer to the owner's exclusivity requirement.

## 4. Legal/practical exclusivity

- **What leaks with open weights**: the architecture (config.json), the tokenizer, and distillable behavior — distillation of open weights is now a recognized industry fault line ([Medium overview](https://medium.com/@graison/large-language-model-distillation-the-new-ai-fault-line-e1fafb99665f), Apr 2026). What never leaks: the data pipeline, raw corpora, RL environments, and eval harnesses.
- **Patents**: labs do patent — OpenAI's first ~11 GenAI patents published 2024 with accelerated grants ([IPKat](https://ipkitten.blogspot.com/2024/08/openais-granted-large-language-model.html)); Google holds the largest AI portfolio; ~1,300 LLM patent families 2014–2023 ([WIPO GenAI landscape](https://www.wipo.int/web-publications/patent-landscape-report-generative-artificial-intelligence-genai/en/2-global-patenting-and-research-in-genai.html)). For a solo-led company, patents are a defensive/valuation asset, not an enforcement tool; trade secret on the data engine is cheaper and stronger.
- **License menu observed among comparable labs**: Apache 2.0 for adoption (Zyphra, OLMo, RWKV, Sarvam); **revenue-gated dual license** for monetized openness (Liquid's LFM license: free under $10M revenue, paid above — [liquid.ai/lfm-license](https://www.liquid.ai/lfm-license)); fully closed + API (Reka pre-partnerships). The Liquid model is the best fit for "exclusivity + visibility."

## 5. Context-length reality check (bears on the 250K requirement)

Native 256K+ at small scale is becoming table stakes, not IP: Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 ships **262,144-token native context** ([HF model card](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507), 2025). But *usable* long context is still rare and measurable: RULER/HELMET/BABILong separate marketing from capability; MegaBeam-7B beat GPT-4-1106 on RULER at 128K ([arXiv 2505.08651](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.08651v1)). A 7B with **verified** RULER/BABILong performance at 256K+ plus agentic tool use would occupy a slot nobody credibly holds in open weights today.
